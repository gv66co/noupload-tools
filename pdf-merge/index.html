How this works

The merging happens in your browser using an open source PDF engine. The files you pick are read from your disk into your browser's memory, combined there, and the result is saved straight back to your downloads folder. There is no upload step, no server, no account and no file size limit beyond your device's memory.

When to use it

Contracts with signature pages, scans that arrived as separate files, chapters of a report, invoices for one claim. Anything you would rather not hand to a random converter site: the whole point of this tool is that nobody else ever sees the document.

Every tool on this site runs inside your browser. Files are processed on your device and are never uploaded to any server. Close the tab and nothing remains.
